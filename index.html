Frequently asked questions

Things you
might ask

What kind of projects do you take on?
Ad films, influencer campaigns and monthly Instagram content for brands. If it needs a story people will remember, it’s our thing.
Do you actually shoot, or is it all AI?
We shoot for real, with real people and real locations. AI comes in for the shots a set can’t give, like a stadium, a new background or an effect.
Can you handle our Instagram every month?
Yes. We plan the month, shoot, edit, write the captions and post. You approve, we handle the rest.
Do you make ads in Kannada and Hindi?
Yes. We make campaigns in Kannada, Hindi and English, so the same story feels local wherever it runs.
Do you work outside Vijayapura?
Yes. We’re based in Vijayapura and work with brands across India.
How do we get started?
Message us on WhatsApp with your brand and what you need. We’ll reply within a day with ideas and next steps.
